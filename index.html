Biography

I am a PhD Research Scholar (JRF) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. My research currently focuses on medical image analysis, where I explore machine learning and computer vision techniques to better understand complex medical data and develop reliable AI systems for healthcare.

In the long run, I hope to bridge artificial intelligence and computer architecture by designing intelligent systems that are not only accurate but also efficient, scalable, and deployable. I am particularly interested in understanding how advances in computing systems, hardware-aware optimization, and algorithm design can make next-generation medical AI faster, more accessible, and ready for real-world clinical applications.

Outside of research, I enjoy building software, exploring how computer systems work from the hardware up, and continuously learning new technologies that connect theory with practical impact.
